Upload & Queue Cadence

Pinterest allows a maximum of 100 pins per CSV file. Master CSVs will be split into batches of this size.

Recommended: 1 batch per run. For example, if a 100-pin batch covers 2 days of pins and your schedule is set to run every 2 days, 1 batch will be uploaded each cycle to safely drip your content over time.

When an account's remaining pending batches reach or fall below this number, you'll receive a Telegram alert to upload a new Master CSV before the schedule runs dry.
