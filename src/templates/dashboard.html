🚀 3-Step Automation Workflow
1. Connect Account

Add your account in Accounts and upload its state.json session generated via the local login script.

2. Upload Master CSV

Drop your master CSV in Upload CSV. The app auto-splits it into ≤100 pin batches and puts them in your queue.

3. Set Schedule Cadence

Choose your run interval in Schedule (e.g. Every 2 days). The app will automatically upload 1 batch per interval.
